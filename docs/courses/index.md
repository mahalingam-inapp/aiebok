# Courses

Each course groups self-contained lectures into sections and ends with a project. For an ordered sequence matched to your role, choose a [learning path](../getting-started/learning-paths/index.md).

## No-code courses

- [AI Literacy](ai-literacy/index.md) — Recognize where AI can help, where it can fail, and how to verify an output before using it.
- [Using AI at Work](using-ai-at-work/index.md) — Write clear requests, review generated work, and design a simple human handoff.
- [AI Product Decisions](ai-product-decisions/index.md) — Turn an AI proposal into a measurable decision about a bounded pilot.
- [Managing AI Delivery](managing-ai-delivery/index.md) — Assign ownership, review launch evidence, and decide when to pause or roll back an AI feature.
- [Evaluating AI in Practice](evaluating-ai-in-practice/index.md) — Compare AI-assisted workflows with an evidence scorecard and a recorded keep, revise, or stop decision.
- [Model Selection Decisions for Leaders](model-selection-for-leaders/index.md) — Use a shared candidate scorecard to make a model purchasing or rollout decision.

## Technical project practicums

- [Build and Evaluate a Model-Backed Application](model-backed-applications/index.md) — Build a source-grounded service desk assistant, measure its performance, and run a provider comparison.
- [Supervised Coding-Agent Harnesses](supervised-coding-harness/index.md) — Build a reviewable harness around a proposed change and measure whether its checks catch regressions.
- [Model Selection and Adaptation Lab](model-selection-and-adaptation/index.md) — Train and compare a small text classifier using governed splits, task slices, cost estimates, and a release decision.
- [Deploy and Operate an AI Service](operating-ai-services/index.md) — Exercise a versioned service, fallback, canary, telemetry, and rollback in a local deployment.
- [Customer Pilot Engineering](customer-pilot-engineering/index.md) — Turn an ambiguous customer request into an accepted, observable pilot with an operator handoff.
- [LLM Evaluation and Model Selection](llm-evaluation-benchmarks/index.md) — Build a paired benchmark, calibrate a grading rubric, and decide which candidate can ship.
- [Retrieval System Engineering](retrieval-system-engineering/index.md) — Build and diagnose a versioned, access-aware retrieval pipeline with lexical and dense rankings.
- [Durable Agent Runtime and MCP](durable-agent-tool-runtime/index.md) — Build a local review workflow with typed tool access, approvals, durable state, and replay-safe execution.
- [LLM Adaptation and Inference Lab](llm-adaptation-inference/index.md) — Adapt an autoregressive model, compare retrieval and tuning, and quantify serving trade-offs.
- [AI Reliability and Release Engineering](ai-reliability-release/index.md) — Operate a staged model release using service objectives, trace diagnosis, and an incident exercise.

## Technical courses

- [01 · Foundations of Intelligence](../books/01-foundations-of-intelligence/index.md) — Understand intelligence as search, learning, representation, decision-making, and feedback before studying language models.
- [02 · Machine Learning Systems](../books/02-machine-learning-systems/index.md) — Learn how models are trained, validated, diagnosed, and integrated into reliable software systems.
- [03 · Language and Representation](../books/03-language-and-representation/index.md) — Understand how language becomes computable representations and why embeddings enable semantic systems.
- [04 · Transformers and Foundation Models](../books/04-transformers-and-foundation-models/index.md) — Understand the architecture, training, inference, and model families behind modern generative AI.
- [05 · Prompt and Context Engineering](../books/05-prompt-and-context-engineering/index.md) — Design the information, instructions, state, and output boundaries that make model behavior useful and testable.
- [06 · Knowledge and Retrieval Systems](../books/06-knowledge-and-retrieval-systems/index.md) — Build grounded knowledge systems whose ingestion, retrieval, generation, and citations can be evaluated separately.
- [07 · Reasoning and Tool Use](../books/07-reasoning-and-tool-use/index.md) — Understand when inference-time search, planning, verification, and external tools improve task outcomes.
- [08 · Agent Systems](../books/08-agent-systems/index.md) — Design agentic systems as bounded stateful architectures rather than treating autonomy as a model feature.
- [09 · AI Software and Product Engineering](../books/09-ai-software-and-product-engineering/index.md) — Use specifications, evaluations, secure development practices, and product discovery to deliver useful AI features.
- [10 · Evaluation, Safety, and Governance](../books/10-evaluation-safety-and-governance/index.md) — Build evidence that AI systems meet task, safety, security, compliance, and business requirements.
- [11 · Training, Serving, and AI Operations](../books/11-training-serving-and-ai-operations/index.md) — Understand model adaptation, efficient inference, deployment, observability, release, and lifecycle operations.
- [12 · Cloud and Enterprise AI Architecture](../books/12-cloud-and-enterprise-ai-architecture/index.md) — Design secure, governed, resilient AI platforms and map them to managed services without losing vendor-neutral reasoning.
- [13 · Multimodal and Frontier Systems](../books/13-multimodal-and-frontier-systems/index.md) — Understand multimodal pipelines and evaluate emerging AI capabilities through enduring principles and evidence.
