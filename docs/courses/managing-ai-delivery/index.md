# Managing AI Delivery

No code · 2 sections · 4 lectures · About 2 hours, plus the project

Assign ownership, review launch evidence, and decide when to pause or roll back an AI feature.

[Start the first lecture](01-find-the-failure-hidden-in-the-average.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary .study-start data-course="managing-ai-delivery" }

## Practice case · Friday's municipal service launch

Sofia owns a pilot that routes resident reports to city departments. The trial includes routine reports about streetlights, alongside accessibility and urgent road hazards. She has one staff reviewer on Friday and needs to decide which parts can launch, who will watch them, and how to stop safely.

This fictional case continues through the lectures.

For a documented comparison, read [When a better-liked model got harder to trust](../../casebook/model-behavior-rollback.md).

Prepare a launch decision for a pilot and practice responding to one simulated incident.

Name an owner, affected groups, approval evidence, pause criteria, and rollback or fallback procedure.
