# Managing AI Delivery

No code · 2 sections · 4 lectures · About 2 hours, plus the project

Assign ownership, review launch evidence, and decide when to pause or roll back an AI feature.

[Start the first lecture](01-find-the-failure-hidden-in-the-average.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## Entry prerequisites

AI Product Decisions or equivalent. No coding required.

## Course outline

### Own quality and risk

- [01 · Find the failure hidden in the average](01-find-the-failure-hidden-in-the-average.md) — Identify a critical user group or circumstance that needs its own evidence.
- [02 · Assign decision and data ownership](02-assign-decision-and-data-ownership.md) — Name who owns release, data access, ongoing quality, and escalation.

### Launch and respond

- [03 · Plan a limited launch and rollback](03-plan-a-limited-launch-and-rollback.md) — Define the trial audience, stop condition, and fallback.
- [04 · Review an incident and decide when to resume](04-review-an-incident-and-decide-when-to-resume.md) — Separate containment, learning, and the relaunch decision.

## Course project

Prepare a launch decision for a pilot and practice responding to one simulated incident.

**How to check your project**

Name an owner, affected groups, approval evidence, pause criteria, and rollback or fallback procedure.
