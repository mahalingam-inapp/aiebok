# AI Product Decisions

No code · 2 sections · 4 lectures · About 2 hours, plus the project

Turn an AI proposal into a measurable decision about a bounded pilot.

[Start the first lecture](01-frame-the-decision-before-selecting-a-model.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## Entry prerequisites

AI Literacy or equivalent. No coding required.

## Course outline

### Find the right problem

- [01 · Frame the decision before selecting a model](01-frame-the-decision-before-selecting-a-model.md) — Describe a business workflow in terms of its user, friction, and outcome.
- [02 · Compare rules, search, and generation](02-compare-rules-search-and-generation.md) — Choose the simplest design that can meet the requirement.

### Set evidence and limits

- [03 · Define success and guardrails](03-define-success-and-guardrails.md) — Write a primary metric and a separate quality threshold for a pilot.
- [04 · Make a ship, revise, or stop decision](04-make-a-ship-revise-or-stop-decision.md) — State a decision based on pilot evidence and remaining uncertainty.

## Course project

Write a proposal for a two-week pilot of an AI feature or workflow.

**How to check your project**

State the problem and baseline, compare alternatives, define a success metric and guardrail, and name the reviewer.
