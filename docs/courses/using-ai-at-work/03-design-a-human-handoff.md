## What you will learn

Write a fallback for incomplete or conflicting information.

## Hand off the problem with context

Facilities confirms the entrance but asks Maya to verify a portable ramp. The publication deadline is in 15 minutes.

Send the reviewer the draft, the confirmed entrance, the unanswered ramp question, and the time the notice must go out. Give the reviewer a safe interim option such as publishing confirmed details with a contact for accessibility questions.

## How it works

A handoff names who reviews the issue, what evidence they receive, and what action waits for their decision. Define the trigger before using the workflow so the same situation gets the same treatment.

### Make a handoff actionable

A handoff should include the state of the work, the open decision, the evidence, the new owner, and the time by which it matters. Simply forwarding a draft forces the next person to reconstruct what has already been checked. They may assume that a polished sentence is approved when it was only proposed.

For the moved workshop, the reviewer needs the confirmed room, the facilities message about the ramp, the publication deadline, and permission to publish a version with the access detail pending. State what has not been sent. This prevents both colleagues from sending separate versions to the same participants.

Choose a useful fallback before a deadline creates pressure. If no reviewer is available, publish only the confirmed details or hold the announcement if the missing information would stop people attending safely. State who will post the update and where. The best handoff lets a person restore the workflow even if the drafting tool is unavailable.

## Worked example

When two handbooks give different leave rules, the assistant can flag the discrepancy and send both dated references to HR. Publication pauses until HR confirms the applicable rule.

## Try it yourself

Design a handoff for a customer notice when two internal documents disagree on the launch date.

??? success "Compare your answer"
    Send both dated records and the draft notice to the launch owner. Hold distribution pending a confirmed date.
