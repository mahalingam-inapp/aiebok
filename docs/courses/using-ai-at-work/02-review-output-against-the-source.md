## What you will learn

Use a short checklist to catch factual and format errors.

## An attractive draft can still fail one line

The draft adds ‘plenty of parking’ and changes the start from 10:30 to 10:00. The colleague says the tone sounds perfect.

Mark claims that could change what a participant does. Trace each one to a source and remove unsupported additions. Review usefulness and factual support separately so a pleasant tone does not hide the wrong start time.

## How it works

Review a draft against its request and original evidence. Check names, dates, numbers, claims, and audience. A polished rewrite can silently change a requirement or remove a caveat.

### Review the result in two passes

First inspect the facts and obligations. Read dates, numbers, places, required steps, and promises against the notes. Check for omissions as well as invented statements. A draft with the right room can still be unusable if it leaves out the only accessible entrance.

In the second pass, read as the recipient. Could someone tell whether a change is confirmed? Do they know where to go or whom to contact? This is a different test from whether the sentences are factually accurate. Keep the two judgments separate so a friendly tone does not raise your confidence in an unsupported instruction.

When the draft says “plenty of parking,” ask which source confirms it. You can delete the claim, soften it only if a reliable source justifies the revision, or ask the responsible person to verify it. Rewriting a guess in more careful language does not solve the evidence problem. Mark unresolved items in the draft until someone owns them.

## Worked example

The source says office access resumes Monday at 10 a.m. A draft says Monday morning. The missing time matters to early arrivals, so restore it.

### Follow the example

**Given:** Source notes say ‘Room 204, Friday at noon, seats assigned by a lottery.’ The draft promises a seat to anyone registering Friday.

**Walkthrough:** Restore noon and the allocation rule. Have the event owner check the room separately. These are distinct factual and audience checks.

## Try it yourself

The source says registration closes Friday at noon and places are limited. A draft says to sign up by Friday and guarantees a seat. List both corrections.
