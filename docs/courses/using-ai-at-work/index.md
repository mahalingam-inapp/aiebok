# Using AI at Work

No code · 2 sections · 4 lectures · About 2 hours, plus the project

Write clear requests, review generated work, and design a simple human handoff.

[Start the first lecture](01-turn-a-request-into-a-clear-brief.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary .study-start data-course="using-ai-at-work" }

## Practice case · The neighborhood workshop moves indoors

Maya coordinates a free weekend workshop. She needs to move it indoors and notify participants in simple language. The new room is confirmed, the accessible entrance is still being checked, and a colleague will review the announcement before publication.

This fictional case continues through the lectures.

For a documented comparison, read [What changed for customer support workers](../../casebook/support-assistants.md).

## Entry prerequisites

AI Literacy or equivalent. All activities can be done on paper or in a document.

## Course project

Improve a low-risk draft and retain a short record of the brief, checks, and edits.

**How to check your project**

Your final draft meets the brief, uses verified facts, and identifies who owns approval.
