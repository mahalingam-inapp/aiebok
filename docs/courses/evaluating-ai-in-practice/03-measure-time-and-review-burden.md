## What you will learn

Calculate the total effort and compare it with the existing workflow.

## Where did the saving go?

The tool saves eight minutes drafting a notice. Ben then spends five minutes verifying details and four minutes making corrections.

This draft took one minute more than the baseline once the extra review work is included. Keep a time column for each stage and count which corrections were consequential. Do not remove aborted drafts from the comparison.

## How it works

Measure drafting, verification, rework, and handoff separately. Track how many outputs required major edits. A faster initial answer can increase total effort if checking and correction are expensive.

### Time the whole task and record correction effort

An assisted draft may arrive quickly while the person who signs it takes longer to check it. Record the start and finish of briefing, drafting, reviewing, fixing, and sending. Include time spent asking someone else for confirmation. A quick failed attempt still consumed time and should stay in the comparison.

Measure quality alongside time. Track how often a material fact had to be corrected and how much work fell on the reviewer. If a tool shifts specialist work into a queue with a fixed capacity, the average time on any one notice may not describe what happens at busy times.

Compare similar requests and keep task types visible. Separate routine reminders from late cancellations. A favorable time result for the first group can support a limited change even if the second group needs the existing workflow. Explain the difference with cases, rather than calling the whole trial a success or failure.

## Worked example

Manual work takes 18 minutes. An assisted version takes 5 minutes to draft, 7 to verify, and 8 to correct. The assisted workflow takes 20 minutes and adds review work.

### Follow the example

**Given:** A candidate saves 20 minutes of drafting and adds 16 minutes of review over five notices.

**Walkthrough:** Report four minutes of total savings and the count of material corrections. Check who absorbs the review time and whether it moves past a deadline.

## Try it yourself

Manual drafting takes 22 minutes. Assisted drafting takes 6 minutes, verification takes 8, and corrections take 3. Compare the time and write one remaining quality check.
