# Make a bounded selection decision

[Model Selection Decisions for Leaders](index.md) · Compare economics and decide · Lecture 4 of 4 · 20–30 min · No code

## What you will learn

Specify a model choice, approval owner, and conditions for reconsidering it.

## How it works

Agree on release gates before comparing results. Among eligible options, choose on the most relevant measured dimensions. Record what new evidence, price change, or rollout failure would alter the decision.

## Worked example

Balanced passes both critical checks and serves under 800 ms. A new regional language cohort could change the choice.

## Try it yourself
