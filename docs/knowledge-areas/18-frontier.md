# KA 18 — Frontier AI

## Content plan

Long context, computer-use agents, world models, continual learning, autonomous software engineering, scientific AI, embodied AI, model-based planning, self-improvement claims, new inference paradigms, and research forecasting.

## Review method

For each claim: define the capability, identify the evidence, reproduce a small result, compare a baseline, inspect limitations, and map it back to enduring concepts in earlier knowledge areas.

!!! warning
    This area is intentionally time-sensitive. Date every page, prefer primary sources, and distinguish demonstrated capability from speculation.
