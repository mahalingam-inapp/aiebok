# KA 00 — Foundations

## Purpose

Build a vocabulary for intelligence, learning, reasoning, memory, feedback, and optimization before treating LLMs as magic.

## Content plan

1. Intelligence as goal-directed behavior
2. Search, planning, knowledge, and symbolic AI
3. Statistical learning and the deep-learning transition
4. Vectors, matrices, probability, statistics, entropy, and optimization
5. Training, inference, generalization, and feedback loops
6. Engineering trade-offs: accuracy, complexity, data, compute, and risk

## Practice

Implement breadth-first search, A*, and a small game-playing agent. Compare explicit search with learned prediction.

## Architecture studio

Given a decision problem, separate what should be deterministic, learned, retrieved, or reviewed by a human.

## Expansion pointers

Add visual explanations of gradient descent and entropy; a history timeline; a math refresher; and case studies showing why expert systems failed or succeeded.
