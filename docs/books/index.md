# Guided Books

The thirteen books are the primary reading path through AIEBOK. Knowledge-area and concept pages remain the reusable reference layer.

1. [**Foundations of Intelligence**](01-foundations-of-intelligence/index.md) — Understand intelligence as search, learning, representation, decision-making, and feedback before studying language models.
2. [**Machine Learning Systems**](02-machine-learning-systems/index.md) — Learn how models are trained, validated, diagnosed, and integrated into reliable software systems.
3. [**Language and Representation**](03-language-and-representation/index.md) — Understand how language becomes computable representations and why embeddings enable semantic systems.
4. [**Transformers and Foundation Models**](04-transformers-and-foundation-models/index.md) — Understand the architecture, training, inference, and model families behind modern generative AI.
5. [**Prompt and Context Engineering**](05-prompt-and-context-engineering/index.md) — Design the information, instructions, state, and output boundaries that make model behavior useful and testable.
6. [**Knowledge and Retrieval Systems**](06-knowledge-and-retrieval-systems/index.md) — Build grounded knowledge systems whose ingestion, retrieval, generation, and citations can be evaluated separately.
7. [**Reasoning and Tool Use**](07-reasoning-and-tool-use/index.md) — Understand when inference-time search, planning, verification, and external tools improve task outcomes.
8. [**Agent Systems**](08-agent-systems/index.md) — Design agentic systems as bounded stateful architectures rather than treating autonomy as a model feature.
9. [**AI Software and Product Engineering**](09-ai-software-and-product-engineering/index.md) — Use specifications, evaluations, secure development practices, and product discovery to deliver useful AI features.
10. [**Evaluation, Safety, and Governance**](10-evaluation-safety-and-governance/index.md) — Build evidence that AI systems meet task, safety, security, compliance, and business requirements.
11. [**Training, Serving, and AI Operations**](11-training-serving-and-ai-operations/index.md) — Understand model adaptation, efficient inference, deployment, observability, release, and lifecycle operations.
12. [**Cloud and Enterprise AI Architecture**](12-cloud-and-enterprise-ai-architecture/index.md) — Design secure, governed, resilient AI platforms and map them to managed services without losing vendor-neutral reasoning.
13. [**Multimodal and Frontier Systems**](13-multimodal-and-frontier-systems/index.md) — Understand multimodal pipelines and evaluate emerging AI capabilities through enduring principles and evidence.
