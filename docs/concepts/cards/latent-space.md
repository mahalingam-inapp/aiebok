# Latent Space

**Purpose:** Reference card for **latent space** used across AIEBOK books and knowledge areas.

## Core explanation

Latent space in generative models compresses images to lower-dimensional representations for efficient editing and generation.

## Example

Latent diffusion edits background without re-encoding full resolution each step.

## Evidence of understanding

Measure edit consistency and artifact rate across ten latent manipulations.

## Trade-offs

No mechanism is universal. Compare latent space against a simpler baseline on normal, boundary, and adversarial cases before adding operational complexity.
