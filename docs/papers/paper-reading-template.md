# Paper Reading Template

## Citation

Title, authors, venue/archive, year, canonical link, date reviewed.

## One-sentence contribution

What changed because of this work?

## Problem and prior art

What limitation existed? What did the strongest previous approach do?

## Core idea

Explain the mechanism with one diagram and a minimal example.

## Evidence

Datasets, baselines, metrics, ablations, compute, and whether the evidence supports the claim.

## Limitations

Assumptions, missing comparisons, external validity, safety, cost, and reproducibility.

## Lasting impact

Which later systems adopted, modified, or rejected the idea? What timeless principle remains?

## Reproduction exercise

Specify the smallest experiment that tests the central claim.
