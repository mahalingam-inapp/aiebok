## Study pace

Allow about two hours per course for the lectures and exercises, plus project work. Use the answer examples to review your work before progressing.

## Completion evidence

Complete an AI-use checklist, a reviewed draft, and a three-case scorecard with a bounded keep, revise, or stop decision.
