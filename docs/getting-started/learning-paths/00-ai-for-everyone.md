# AI for Everyone

No code · 3 courses

People who use or encounter AI at work or in everyday life.

## Entry prerequisites

No programming, AI background, or AI account required.

## Course sequence

1. [AI Literacy](../../courses/ai-literacy/index.md)
2. [Using AI at Work](../../courses/using-ai-at-work/index.md)
3. [Evaluating AI in Practice](../../courses/evaluating-ai-in-practice/index.md)

## Study pace

Allow about two hours per course for the lectures and exercises, plus project work. Use the answer examples to review your work before progressing.

## Completion evidence

Complete an AI-use checklist, a reviewed draft, and a three-case scorecard with a bounded keep, revise, or stop decision.
