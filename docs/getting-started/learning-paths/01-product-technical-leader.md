# Product & technical leader

**Duration:** ~8 weeks · **Steps:** 17 · **Complexity rank:** 1 of 5

Product managers and technical leaders who need capability literacy, risk framing, and build-versus-buy judgment—not daily implementation.

**Outcome:** Explain model boundaries, evaluation gates, responsible-AI trade-offs, and enterprise adoption patterns to engineering and business stakeholders.
