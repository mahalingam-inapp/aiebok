# AI Reliability & Release Engineer

Advanced specialist · operations · 5 courses

Platform, ML operations, and reliability engineers operating model-backed features.

## Entry prerequisites

HTTP, service telemetry, and basic evaluation. Complete the service practicum before the reliability lab.

## Course sequence

1. [Evaluation, Safety, and Governance](../../books/10-evaluation-safety-and-governance/index.md)
2. [Training, Serving, and AI Operations](../../books/11-training-serving-and-ai-operations/index.md)
3. [Cloud and Enterprise AI Architecture](../../books/12-cloud-and-enterprise-ai-architecture/index.md)
4. [Deploy and Operate an AI Service](../../courses/operating-ai-services/index.md)
5. [AI Reliability and Release Engineering](../../courses/ai-reliability-release/index.md)

## Study pace

Plan for 10–12 hours per technical core course, 3–4 hours per practicum, and about two hours per no-code course, plus project work. Use the core course checkpoints to prepare for the applied workbooks. Budget additional time for the capstone.

## Completion evidence

Diagnose a staged release failure, restore the baseline, and produce an evidence-based incident review. Follow the [capstone brief and rubric](ai-reliability-capstone.md).
