# Retrieval & RAG Engineer

Specialist · information retrieval · 5 courses

Engineers responsible for enterprise search, knowledge-grounded features, and citation quality.

## Entry prerequisites

Know basic embeddings and Python. Take Language and Representation if you need the vector search foundations.

## Study pace

Plan for 10–12 hours per technical core course, 3–4 hours per practicum, and about two hours per no-code course, plus project work. Use the core course checkpoints to prepare for the applied workbooks. Budget additional time for the capstone.

## Completion evidence

Demonstrate a versioned, permission-aware retrieval pipeline and quantify its quality by stage. Follow the [capstone brief and rubric](retrieval-rag-capstone.md).
