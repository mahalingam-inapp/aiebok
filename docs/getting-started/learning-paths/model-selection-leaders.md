# Model Selection for Leaders

No code · specialist · 3 courses

Product owners, program leads, and sponsors making a model or vendor decision.

## Entry prerequisites

Basic AI literacy and familiarity with pilot decisions, or start with AI Literacy.

## Course sequence

1. [AI Product Decisions](../../courses/ai-product-decisions/index.md)
2. [Evaluating AI in Practice](../../courses/evaluating-ai-in-practice/index.md)
3. [Model Selection Decisions for Leaders](../../courses/model-selection-for-leaders/index.md)

## Study pace

Allow about two hours per course for the lectures and exercises, plus project work. Use the answer examples to review your work before progressing. Budget additional time for the capstone.

## Completion evidence

Submit a recommendation that ties one candidate to quality, economics, and launch conditions. Follow the [capstone brief and rubric](model-selection-leaders-capstone.md).
