# ML engineer

**Duration:** ~16 weeks · **Steps:** 45 · **Complexity rank:** 4 of 5

Machine-learning engineers focused on training pipelines, embeddings, transformers, serving, and operational evaluation—not product UX.

**Outcome:** Deliver a fine-tuned or adapted model with profiling, slice evaluation, serving plan, and LLMOps monitoring hooks.

!!! tip "Share this path"
    Send this page URL to teammates who need the **ML engineer** track.
