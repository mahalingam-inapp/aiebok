# Model Architecture & Decision Systems Engineer

Advanced specialist · models and policies · 3 courses

ML, inference, and decision systems engineers working on model internals, serving choices, or decisions driven by predictions.

## Entry prerequisites

Python, probability, and transformer fundamentals. Review [Machine Learning Systems](../../books/02-machine-learning-systems/index.md) and [Transformers and Foundation Models](../../books/04-transformers-and-foundation-models/index.md) if needed.

## Study pace

Allow 3–4 hours per practicum for lectures and notebook exercises, plus project work. Budget additional time for the capstone.

## Completion evidence

Compare expert capacity and inference budgets, then ship a reproducible synthetic policy evaluation with a human handoff and release gate. Follow the [capstone brief and rubric](model-architecture-decision-capstone.md).
